NEURAL ASSEMBLY — NATIVE BLENDER FILM

30 seconds; 1920 × 1080; 24 fps; H.264/AAC.
26 seconds of progressive branch assembly during a full 360-degree camera orbit,
followed by a four-second hold. The website adds the supplied Higgsfield / GPT-6
Astra badge as a responsive overlay; social exports include it in the video.

Frames were rendered from source-derived Blender geometry, not regenerated by an
AI video model. The reveal is editorial timing, not recorded biological growth.
Traveling lights follow 96 verified paths within 32 individual neurons. Timing and
direction are illustrative, not measured activity or transmission across synapses.
The soundtrack was created with Sonilo through Higgsfield.

The web overview contains simplified source centerlines and measured anchors;
complete source skeletons are available on demand for all 166,700 catalog IDs.
These are not full neuron membrane surfaces. Colors, labels, and display scale are visualization choices.
A skeleton centroid is a navigation anchor, not a measured soma position.
Unknown annotations remain unknown; cell types do not establish all functions.

The catalog contains 166,700 classified records; the paper reports 166,691 neurons.
The reason for this nine-record difference has not been established here.
This is a fruit fly dataset, not a human connectome. The project is not endorsed
by the original research teams. See the repository attribution and license files
for software, research-data, and branding terms.
